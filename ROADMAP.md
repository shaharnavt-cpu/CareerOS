# CareerOS Roadmap

This roadmap summarizes the planned delivery phases. Product requirements and
accepted architecture decisions in `docs/` remain the authoritative source.

## Phase 1 — Project foundation

- Establish the monorepo and contributor workflow.
- Scaffold the Next.js frontend, FastAPI backend, and extension boundary.
- Provide repeatable local setup, linting, builds, and tests.

## Phase 2 — Career knowledge foundation

- Define canonical, editable career knowledge.
- Model supporting evidence and provenance as first-class concepts.
- Establish persistence, migrations, and application boundaries.

## Phase 3 — Core application workflow

- Analyze a manually supplied job description.
- Generate evidence-backed resume and cover-letter drafts.
- Support human review and immutable submission snapshots.

## Phase 4 — Application lifecycle

- Track mutable application stages and timeline events.
- Record interviews, assessments, offers, outcomes, and reflections.
- Introduce basic, explainable outcome analytics.

## Phase 5 — Browser extension and integrations

- Add read-only job extraction before any autofill capability.
- Introduce supported job-site adapters and manual fallbacks.
- Add email, calendar, and storage integrations incrementally.

## Phase 6 — Scale and intelligence

- Expand analytics and strategy recommendations.
- Add multi-user support and collaboration where justified.
- Improve provider flexibility, evaluation, privacy controls, and operations.
